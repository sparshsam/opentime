# About OpenTime

> Calm, privacy-first desktop time companion. Clocks, world clocks, and more.
> *Part of the [Kovina](https://kovina.org) ecosystem.*

---

## What This App Is

OpenTime is a Utilities application designed for desktop. OpenTime is a calm, privacy-first, local-first desktop time companion. It places clock widgets directly on the Windows desktop. Future versions will add calendars, alarms, timers, and reminders.

It is forged with care for people who value:

- **Ownership** — You control the software and your data.
- **Permanence** — Built to last, not to be replaced next quarter.
- **Clarity** — Straightforward purpose, honest design, no dark patterns.
- **Privacy** — Local-first by default, surveillance-free by design.

Licensed under AGPL-3.0 — free as in freedom, not just free as in price.

---

## What This App Is Not

- **Not a platform** — OpenTime does not lock you in. Your data uses open formats.
  You can leave anytime without losing anything.
- **Not surveillance** — No telemetry, no analytics, no data mining, no "engagement"
  optimization. The product is the software, not you.
- **Not a subscription** — No recurring fees, no rent-seeking, no "pay to unlock
  what you already have." You buy it (or build it) once.
- **Not a startup** — This is not a growth-hacked venture looking for an exit.
  It is software that solves a problem and stays solved.

## Kovina Philosophy

This project follows the Kovina design principles:

2. **Built to be understood** — Clear purpose, transparent behavior, honest
   communication. No dark patterns, no hidden features, no surprises.
