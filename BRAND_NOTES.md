## Tone

OpenTime follows the **Kovina editorial voice**: calm, editorial, confident.

- **Calm** — No hype, no urgency, no exclamation points in copy. Readable sentences.
- **Editorial** — Precise language, consistent terminology, thoughtful pacing.
- **Confident** — Speaks with authority earned by substance. No hedging, no qualifiers.
